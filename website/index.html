FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sovereign Resource DAO?

A decentralized autonomous organization that uses AI agents and blockchain technology to protect African mining communities from exploitation. We track minerals, audit contracts, and ensure fair revenue distribution.

How does the Extraction Fairness Index work?

The index compares what miners actually receive against global market rates, factoring in transportation, processing costs, and regional benchmarks. A score of 100 means miners receive full fair market value.

Is the app free to use?

Yes. The app is completely free and open source. There are no hidden fees, no premium tiers, and no data harvesting. Communities own the platform.

Which countries are currently supported?

We currently operate in 14 African nations including DRC, Ghana, Tanzania, Zambia, Mozambique, and others. We're expanding based on community demand.

How is my data protected?

All sensitive data is encrypted end-to-end. Blockchain records are pseudonymous. The app collects only what's necessary for mineral tracking and never sells data to third parties.

Can I contribute to the project?

Absolutely. The entire codebase is open source on GitHub. We welcome contributions in code, translation, community organizing, and governance participation.
