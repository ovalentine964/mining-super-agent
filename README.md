## AI-Powered Mineral Exploration & Market Intelligence Platform

**Built for Kenyan miners. Powered by NVIDIA's Superagent Blueprint. Designed to end mineral exploitation.**

---

## 🎯 What Is This?

A free, AI-powered digital geologist that lives in your phone. It gives miners the same geological and market data that foreign companies use to exploit them — so for the first time, miners can negotiate from a position of knowledge instead of ignorance.

**The problem:** Chinese mining companies test your land, find gold worth billions, and offer you 1 million KES. You accept because you don't know what's underneath.

**The solution:** An AI system that tells you what's on your land, what it's worth, and what a fair deal looks like — for free, in Swahili, via Telegram.
