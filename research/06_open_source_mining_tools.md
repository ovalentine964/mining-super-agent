### Key Remote Sensing Indices for Mineral Exploration

- **NDVI** — vegetation stress (indicator of subsurface mineralization)
- **Band ratios** (Sentinel-2 B11/B12) — clay/hydroxyl mapping
- **Fe-oxide ratio** (B4/B2) — iron oxide detection (gossan indicators)
- **AlOH/Hydroxyl** (B6/B7) — alteration zone mapping
- **PCA** on multispectral bands — anomaly detection
