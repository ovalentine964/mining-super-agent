4. Legal Information

Kenya Mining Act 2016

Under the Kenya Mining Act (2016), all mining activities require a license from the Cabinet Secretary for Mining. License types include:

  • Prospecting License — To explore for minerals
  • Mining License — To extract minerals
  • Dealer's License — To trade in minerals

Landowner Rights

As a landowner, you have the right to:

  • Know the mineral value of your land
  • Receive fair compensation before mining begins
  • Participate in negotiations with miners
  • Obtain independent analysis reports
